ONE

Total supply: 1 token. Not one billion. One.

Most memecoins have a supply of a trillion. This one has a supply of 1. It divides into 1018 pieces, so you buy a fraction of the only ONE that exists.

Liquidity is about one dollar. This is a joke that happens to be on-chain, not an investment. Buy a fraction if you think it's funny. Don't buy it if you want money.

How to buy

  1. Have a TON wallet with some TON in it (Tonkeeper works).
  2. Open the DeDust link above and connect your wallet. If ONE isn't listed, paste the contract address into the token search — it's unverified, so it won't show up on its own.
  3. Raise the slippage in settings. The pool is tiny, so any buy moves the price a lot and the swap will fail on default settings.
  4. Swap. You'll get a very small number. That's the point — the whole supply is one token.

Facts

  1. Mint is revoked — the admin is the zero address, so no more can ever be created.
  2. Liquidity is permanently locked. It can't be pulled.
  3. Market cap equals the price of one token, because the supply is one.
